# T2 - primers

Every primer used in this work, as ordered, 5'->3'. Oligo-pool amplification, read-out RT-PCR, Sanger verification.

Where a primer anneals on the minus strand the sequence given is the oligonucleotide, not the plus-strand site it reads; the site is in the position column. Recovery of each sequence is recorded in `T2_findings.md`.
