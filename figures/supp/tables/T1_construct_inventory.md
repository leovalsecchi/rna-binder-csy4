# T1 - construct inventory

Every plasmid built or used in this work: the entry and scaffold plasmids, the screening plasmids, the 32 ordered candidates and the helper plasmids used but not built here. Ordered synthesis fragments are **not** listed; the plasmids assembled from them are.

`size_bp` is the full plasmid length throughout. Every value is measured from the DNA record rather than from a journal label.

**Vectors supplied by a collaborator are redacted**, because their designs are not ours to publish. Their eight rows carry the marker in place of the insert, the length and the type IIS counts; elsewhere their names and architecture are removed from the insert descriptions, and the length is withheld for every construct built on one of them, since that length is mostly theirs. Identifiers are kept throughout, so a reader tracing a construct back to its backbone finds the row and sees where it stops.
